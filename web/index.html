Questions

Is Hirivo really free?

Yes. Everything works with free resources: the public job listings, free AI keys from Groq or Gemini (or Chrome's on-device AI), and your own Gmail. There is no Hirivo server to pay for, so there is nothing to charge you for.

Can my LinkedIn account get banned?

Hirivo never automates LinkedIn. It never clicks, connects, messages or applies there. Searches read LinkedIn's public, logged-out job listings, and it reads a LinkedIn page only when you open it and ask. Connection notes are written for you to copy and send yourself.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

By default it fills the application and you press Submit. If you switch on Autopilot, it can email and apply for the jobs you hand it, with limits: only verified email addresses, a daily cap, never a consent box or CAPTCHA, never LinkedIn, and every step logged in Pipeline.

Do I need an AI key?

No for search, scam and real-job checks, keyword match and form filling. A free key makes fit scores, emails and written answers better; getting a Groq key takes about a minute and Hirivo walks you through it.

Where does my resume go?

Into your browser's own storage, and nowhere else. AI requests go straight from your browser to the provider you chose, with your key. Read the full privacy policy.

Does it work outside India?

Yes. Remote boards, YC startups and company career pages work anywhere, and the form filler handles international ATS. The portal search and some form answers (CTC in LPA, 10th and 12th marks) are tuned for India.
